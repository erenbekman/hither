Claude Code, in the loop

  • Know when it waits. A session asking for permission shows up in Agents and sends a notification.
  • Start in the right place. "Open in Claude" opens claude in your terminal (iTerm, Terminal, Ghostty or Warp), in the repository's folder.
  • Prompts carry ids, not text. A repository, a pull request number, a Sentry issue. Never titles, logs or error text.
  • Nothing runs on its own. Claude starts interactively, with your normal permissions, when you press the button.

Your data stays on your Mac

Your tokens, your requests

Hither talks only to GitHub, Forge and Sentry with your own credentials. There is no Hither server.

Keychain, not files

Forge and Sentry tokens live in the macOS Keychain. GitHub comes from gh auth token.

Claude only on request

Nothing is sent to Claude unless you press a button that says so. Summaries use read-only tools.

Local hooks

The Claude Code hook listener accepts connections from 127.0.0.1 only and checks a random token.
